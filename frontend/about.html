About PeopleIKnow

PeopleIKnow is a simple web app designed to help you keep track of the people you know.

this app was made by Maxwell Christopher Chay who still at the 11th grade. and wanted to be continue studying computer science.

this app was made using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. and the backend was made using Node.js and Express.js. also for the database, I used MongoDB.

the purpose of this app is to help people who met a lot of people and simply named them as soon as possible.

this app is still in development and will be updated regularly.
